# Talmeez — Scope

Last updated: 19 November 2026

## The product in one line
A parent types one sentence and finds a tutor in Lebanon who teaches their child's exact class, with ID-checked tutors.

## Riskiest guess
Parents will search and message tutors on Talmeez instead of asking in WhatsApp groups, and tutors will pay once they get leads.

## In scope now
- Search by one sentence in Arabic, Arabizi, French or English, with editable chips.
- Class matching across programs (EB9 = Brevet = Grade 9), exact matches first, then same class in another program.
- One-line reason per match.
- Messaging that flags phone numbers and agreed bookings, never blocks.
- Lead counting, 2 free leads per tutor, then subscription.
- Identity check for tutors (done by a person).
- Evals and the weekly AI report.

## Later
- Tutor sign-up by talking.
- Admin copilot (photo vs ID pre-check).
- Expo mobile app.

## Out of scope
- Payments inside the site (plans are activated by hand at launch).
- Checking diplomas (only identity is checked).
- Session-replay trackers.

## The number: weekly leads
- **Definition:** a parent sends at least 2 messages and the tutor replies, once per pair. No booking needed.
- **Today:** 0. The new site that counts leads is not live yet. The old site had 3 session requests in total (April, May, September 2026), none since.
- **Done when:** the new site is live and the weekly leads number shows on the admin Insights page.
