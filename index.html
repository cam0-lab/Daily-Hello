Daily Hello

A friendly call for your parent, every single day.

Daily Hello rings your parent on the phone they already have, has a real conversation, and tells you how they're doing. If something sounds wrong, you know right away.

  • No app for your parent
  • No devices to install
  • Their own language
  • Learns each parent's normal
  • Summary after every call
